Transport & Arrival Guide

Wembley Stadium is a public transport destination. Travel via the London Underground, London Overground, or National Rail networks for the smoothest access.

  • Wembley Park Station: Jubilee and Metropolitan lines (5 min direct walk down Olympic Way).
  • Wembley Stadium Station: Chiltern Railways (direct rail connection from London Marylebone).
  • Wembley Central Station: Bakerloo Line & London Overground (15 min pedestrian route).
